First 90 Days

Immediate 90-Day Priorities

The founding priorities that turn HOA’s organizing work into a functioning institution.

1
Complete the organizing-document amendment and federal 501(c)(3) application preparation; address charitable-solicitation compliance and maintain accurate public status language.
2
Hold the formal organizational Board meeting; adopt bylaws, resolutions, officer assignments, committee charters, conflict-of-interest rules, financial controls, youth-safety procedures, and records systems.
3
Open and operate the HOA bank account with strict separation of organizational and personal funds, documented approvals, receipts, and regular financial reporting.
4
Launch a founding sponsorship and fundraising campaign supported by a professional introduction package, program sponsorship options, and a measurable 90-day startup budget.
5
Conduct the first homeowner-stability / probate and heirs’ property education class with qualified partners and document resident needs, referrals, and follow-up.
6
Launch a Community Resource & Opportunity Clinic or partner roundtable with sign-in, needs assessment, referrals, volunteer assignments, and an after-action report.
7
Prepare the next Project LIFT working session and draft MOU scope around outreach, business incubation, arts and cultural engagement, resource connection, reporting, and phased facility access.
8
Host or prepare a youth, workforce, skilled-trades, aviation, and entrepreneurship opportunity event with partner participation and measurable youth/resident outcomes.
9
Begin structured oral-history, historic-photo, church-history, and public-art planning with releases, documentation standards, and community input.
10
Develop a Community Land Trust feasibility roadmap identifying legal structure, resident governance, potential sites, public and private funding, acquisition strategy, and long-term affordability requirements.
