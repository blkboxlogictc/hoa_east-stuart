Founding Progress

July 2026
Institutional Launch Edition
Completed Florida nonprofit incorporation and secured an Employer Identification Number (EIN).
Established a five-director governance framework and prepared bylaws, board resolutions, core policies, committee charters, financial controls, and organizational records for formal adoption.
Convened community workshops and organizing meetings and created committee-specific WhatsApp channels as the primary system for updates, document distribution, requests, brainstorming, and follow-up.
Built working relationships with Project LIFT, the 10th Street Community Center, CRA leadership, United Way leadership, Witham Field and aviation contacts, MartinArts, NAACP connections, churches, housing professionals, and other local partners.
Developed a phased Project LIFT community-benefit partnership framework focused on outreach, business incubation, arts and cultural engagement, resource connection, and future written agreements.
Advanced a homeowner-stability and probate education concept with Nikki Hawkins / Jumpstart Treasure Coast and a probate attorney for future East Stuart classes and a potential United Way presentation.
Prepared youth, workforce, resource-clinic, oral-history, public-art, and cultural-corridor concepts with measurable participation and outcome tracking.
Began exploring a Community Land Trust and land-stewardship pathway as a long-term anti-displacement and affordable-housing strategy.
