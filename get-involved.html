Partner With Us

HOA is seeking partners who understand that strong programs require strong infrastructure. Early support will help the organization build compliance, governance, records, volunteer systems, program pilots, resident trust, and measurable outcomes from the beginning.

Unrestricted institutional launch funding
Support incorporation follow-through, IRS filing, insurance, banking, technology, communications, printing, records, and essential administrative capacity.
Program sponsorships
Sponsor resource clinics, youth and workforce events, homeowner-stability workshops, oral-history activities, public art, community meetings, or volunteer operations.
Professional and technical assistance
Provide legal, accounting, insurance, grant, evaluation, technology, data, historic-preservation, real-estate, housing, and organizational-development expertise.
Facilities and in-kind resources
Provide meeting rooms, printing, food, supplies, equipment, transportation support, outreach, media, photography, translation, or volunteer assistance.
Grant and capacity partnerships
Provide funder introductions, letters of support, joint applications, fiscal sponsorship when appropriate, data, program partners, and measurable collaboration.
Land and housing strategy
Support Community Land Trust feasibility, acquisition planning, due diligence, public subsidy strategy, affordable-housing expertise, and resident-centered governance development.
